The Truth No One Tells You

The people most consumed
by the illness are the least
likely to make contact.

Contact does not work the way the grimoires say it does. She appears as a mirror of your inner state. Desperation, obsession, unresolved grief -- these are what she reflects back. The prepared initiate encounters her. The unprepared encounters their own darkness, amplified beyond what most minds can hold.

⚠️ The Most Common Fatal Mistake

Running. When contact occurs, the experience is beyond what words prepare you for. The sounds are real. The visuals are real. The instinct to flee will be overwhelming. If you run -- if you flee an entity that came out of respect -- the consequences can be severe, lasting, and in the worst cases irreversible. I have witnessed both outcomes. This guide exists so you understand what is at stake before you open that door.
